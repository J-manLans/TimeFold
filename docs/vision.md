# Vision

The project's north star: where it is heading, settled with the owner once the direction is clear, and changed
rarely. No state: nothing about how things are today, what to build next or how far the project has come (that is
the roadmap), and no details of how it works (those are decisions). Read before giving a design opinion.

## Who it is for

The owner is a janitor at a Swedish-language school in Finland, with admin roles beside it and other paid work: a
seat in the school's development group, and more as it comes. Today the hours live in an Excel file: a start time on
arrival, a stop time and any time away at the end of the day, and a front page showing whether there are hours to
take out or hours to work in. The app replaces that file, on the owner's work computer and at home.

## Where it is heading

A personal time clock, and a small dashboard the owner likes looking at.

- **One click to start, one to stop.** Recording work is a single action, quick enough never to be skipped.
- **An honest record.** Time away comes off the day, a forgotten click is written in afterwards, and the record
  remembers which times were set by hand.
- **The balance always in view.** Hours to take out or to work in, and how much of the month's room for other work is
  used, read at a glance from two figures.
- **Every kind of work in its place.** Each kind of work is a profile, counted apart; the janitor job carries the
  balance, and the other work shares what is left of full time.
- **A report the boss reads as it is.** A month on a page, with the same figures as the screen.
- **The owner's alone.** One user, two machines, no internet and no account; the record moves between the machines
  in one file the owner carries.
- **A little flair.** A calm, technical look with one hot accent, motion where it means something, and colours the
  owner chooses.

## What it is not

A team tool, a task or project tracker, a payroll or invoicing system, or anything that needs the internet. It counts
hours; the only money in it is a rough estimate for the owner's own eyes.

## Questions to ask of a new idea

1. Does starting and stopping stay one click?
2. Does it work with no connection, with the data kept only on the owner's machines?
3. Does it make the record more trustworthy, or easier to correct?
4. Would the boss understand the report without the owner explaining it?
5. Does it add something the owner must think about every day? If so, it needs a strong reason.
6. Does it keep the screen calm: one meaning per colour, motion only where it tells something?
