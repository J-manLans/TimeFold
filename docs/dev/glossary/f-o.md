# Glossary: F–O

Terms F–O, in alphabetical order. The index and the rules for terms are in `README.md`.

**Faded.** On the ceiling wheel, the tone of hours worked by another profile than the one on screen: the base or neon in
the same hue, weaker and a little toward the panel's background. Not the dimmed colour, which is the unused part ("The
ceiling wheel", in `design-decisions/`). See **Lit**.

**Flex bar.** The main screen's figure for the balance: a horizontal bar either side of a zero line, with the balance as
a number ("The flex bar", in `design-decisions/`).

**Flex day.** A whole day off paid for with the balance: it takes the expected hours off the balance. Marked in the
calendar in the menu's Tid tab, a weekday per click, ahead or, behind its padlock, past, and unmarked the same way;
"Flexledig" there and in the report. Not annual leave, which is left empty.

**Focus outline.** The ring around whatever has keyboard focus, in the colour of where the profile on screen stands (see
**Standing**). Not the running ring around "Stoppa jobb", which is always the neon.

**Full time.** The hours of a full working day (8), a setting; janitor's expected hours and the ceiling divide it.

**Gap.** The time between a stop and the next start, or cut from an entry on the entry line. Not work; it counts on no
day.

**Handover.** Moving the record from one of the owner's machines to the other: export one file on the first, import it
on the second, replacing everything there.

**Hue (a profile's).** Where the hue strip of the profile's colour picker sits; the base colour is it muted and the neon
its opposite ("Each profile carries its colours", in `design-decisions/`).

**Light sweep.** At the onboarding's end, the pointer light crossing the button block once by itself, in an arc, before
the letter arrives. In the code, the `--onboarding-sweep` tokens and `sweep()`. Not the **docs sweep** in `AGENTS.md`.

**Lit.** On the ceiling wheel, hours worked by the profile on screen, in its base or neon at full strength. See
**Faded**.

**Locked panel.** The menu's panel held open over the controls side, with no way out, for the **Onboarding** or the
**Recovery screen**; solid while it asks, then frost, then folded into the menu button.

**Month.** The app's counting month: the calendar month, the 1st to the last day; the ceiling, reports, the pay estimate
and pruning count by it.

**Month line.** In the "Visa alla" popup, the quiet line where the list crosses into a month, naming it and its year
("november 2026"). The recent list has none.

**Motion.** The app's own setting for whether anything moves: **Animerat** or **Stilla** (nothing moves), set with the
motion switch beside the theme button and following the system's reduced-motion setting until it is first clicked; in
the settings file.

**Neon.** A profile's colour for "more than expected": hours over the ceiling, a plus balance, and the numbers that show
them; the opposite of the profile's hue at its most vivid, never picked. The one hot accent.

**Onboarding.** The first start on a machine with no record: "Starta nytt" or "Importera", then the balance and the
profile's name, in the **Locked panel**, ending with the **Light sweep** and the letter arriving.

**Opening figures.** Figures brought over from before the app, kept in the record apart from the entries and dated the
day they start from: the janitor balance, typed in when the app starts fresh, and the ceiling hours already used, whose
asking is still open; the balance and the ceiling count from them.
