# Glossary: P–Z

Terms P–Z, in alphabetical order. The index and the rules for terms are in `README.md`.

**Padlock.** In the flex-day calendar, the small lock beside the month name: opened, days gone by can be marked or
unmarked as flex days; it shuts again when the menu closes. A weekend and a day worked stay locked either way.

**Pay kind.** How a profile is paid: the profile with the balance a monthly salary (Månadslön), every other profile a
rate per hour (Timlön). Shown after the profile's name on the report.

**Pay estimate.** A rough figure of the month's pay: salaries plus hours times rates, for the owner only; never in the
report.

**Peek.** Resting the pointer on something to see what it hides: on the ceiling wheel's band, which winds the over lap
back so the first lap shows whole; on the "Visa alla" card behind the edit popup, which fades the edit so the list reads
through.

**Pointer light.** The soft light that follows the pointer over the controls side, lying only on the buttons and discs
that can be used ("The look: a fresh display face, mono text and soft buttons").

**Profile.** A label on an entry for the kind of work it is, such as janitor work or the development group. Profiles are
isolated; only one with expected hours has a balance.

**Prototype.** The app's screens as tuned before they are built, `docs/vision/prototype.html`: the north star for the
look beside the vision, kept until the finished app has been validated. Not a **Sketch**.

**Prune.** Removing the record's oldest whole months, past three years, on the owner's yes; the balance and ceiling
hours at the cut become opening figures.

**Reason (an edit's).** Why an entry's time or profile was set by hand, chosen under the entry line from the list, which
learns a text written three times and is edited under each reason's pen, or written after "Annan orsak". Stored as text
on the entry.

**Recovery screen.** What the app opens on when its record cannot be read: the newest **Daily copy** offered, shown on
the dashboard under a breathing "KOPIA" label; with no copy, a fresh start; for a newer record, an update.

**Release.** A version of the app built by GitHub from a version tag, such as `v0.1.0`, with installers for Windows and
Linux on the repo's releases page ("The app reaches each machine as an installer from a GitHub release", in
`design-decisions/`). Not the record's version, which numbers the file's shape.

**Report.** A period's record, exported from the app for the owner to send to their boss.

**Settings file.** Each machine's own `settings.json` beside the record, holding what belongs to that computer: the
theme and the motion; never in the record or the handover.

**Spill (a break's).** In a divided entry, the part of the break that no longer fits its part moving to the other, as a
drag shrinks it; only until a tag is set, after which each part keeps its own.

**Stacked.** The main screen in a window too narrow for the two sides beside each other: the dashboard over the controls
side, scrolling together as one page. The sides stack once the controls would be narrower than the button block with its
room on both sides. Called **narrow** in the prototype.

**Standing (a profile's).** Where a profile's figure is: past its line (the balance in plus, or the ceiling passed) or
not (at 0 or below). Its letter, its small circle and the focus outline wear it, the neon past the line and the base
before it.

**Shared look.** A look more than one part of the screen wears, written once as a class, an attachment or a component:
the tag, the small button, the caps heading, the fields.

**Sketch.** A quick try of one look before it is decided, in `docs/dev/sketches/`; what it settles goes to its decision,
and it goes once that is built. Not the **Prototype**.

**Small deviations.** In a report, the days whose net change is under 5 minutes, left off the list of days and summed in
its closing line, "Små avvikelser under månaden".

**Surface.** A part of the screen with its own colours that the shared looks sit on: the controls side, the dashboard,
the menu, a popup. Each names its colours under the same five names.

**Type set.** One of the four text styles all text outside the figures wears: heading, body, label and caps.

**Today's pie.** The small ring of slices beside the status line: one per hour the profile with the balance is expected
to work today, filling as each is worked, live, and the neon lapping over it past them.

**Tip.** The small label that follows the pointer over a button, to its right, edged in a profile's colour: what the
undo would undo, or what the start button would stop. With keyboard focus it shows under the button.

**Walk (an entry).** On the entry line, to move an entry past a neighbour of its own profile: dock onto it, stretch past
it, and bring the other handle over; the neighbour is let go whole and the entry carries on beyond it.
