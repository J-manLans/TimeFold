# Main screen: the flex bar

The janitor balance as a number and a bar from its 0 line. Each decision follows the template in `../../README.md`, and
is cited by its title.

## The flex bar (2026-11-15)

**Built by:** Profiles, for the balance profile's colours as chosen in the menu.

**Decision:** the janitor balance shows as a horizontal bar from a 0 line, minus to the left and plus to the right, with
the balance as a number above it. The bar is only its active part, with no track behind it, and is rounded only at its
outer end, square against the 0 line. It is half as thick as the ceiling wheel's band, centred on the 0 line, which
keeps its full height and stands out above and below it. It takes the colours of the profile with the balance ("Each
profile carries its colours"): minus (hours to work in) in its base colour; plus (hours to take out) in its neon;
exactly 0 a marker centred on the 0 line, a little wider than a thin mark, in the base colour dimmed, slowly and
slightly growing and shrinking; the motion stops when motion is off ("Motion can be switched off in the app"). The
number above follows the bar: base colour for minus, neon for plus, the main text colour at exactly 0. The bar's full
width is one of four scales, ±10, ±20, ±40 or ±80 h, with ten small marks on each side from the 0 line to the scale's
end, the end mark taller and labelled in small text; each side's end mark and its label wear that side's colour (minus
the base, plus the over), the other marks stay grey. The scale is the smallest whose 90% holds the balance. Crossing
into a larger scale changes it with a short animation; it goes back down only once the balance is within 80% of the
smaller scale, so it does not flicker at a boundary. Beyond ±80 h the bar stays full at its end. A large minus gets no
warning.

**Why:** one meaning per colour: the neon is "more than expected", which a plus balance is; minus is work still to do,
in the calm base colour; dimmed is "nothing either way". The bar is always janitor work, so it always wears the balance
profile's colours. With no track, the dimmed marker at 0 stands on its own, and its slow pulse reads as at rest. The
coloured end marks keep both of the profile's colours on screen at any balance, each where it means something, so the
pair is seen together and the number is free to follow the bar, as the wheel's number follows its fill. Fixed scales
keep the bar's length meaningful (half the bar is half the labelled amount) while a balance saved up for a holiday week
still fits.

**Rejected:** the name "balance bar": the bar measures flex time. A track behind the bar in the dimmed colour: the 0
marker would vanish into it. Minus in the neon: the neon means hours beyond what is owed, which minus is not. The number
in the colour opposite the bar's, to show both colours: a minus in red reads as a warning, and red would mean "over"
everywhere but there. Every mark in its side's colour: busy. Colours of the bar's own, shared with the wheel through a
"Förena färger" switch: the colours belong to the profiles. A warning for a large minus: the owner does not want one.
One fixed width cut off at its end: past it, the length means nothing. A scale always fitted to the balance: the bar
would always look full.

**Revisit if:** the scale changes feel jumpy in daily use (a compressed far end, linear near 0, was the alternative).

**Cases:**

- Balance 0: the dimmed marker sits centred on the 0 line.
- Balance −3:00: a bar to the left in Vaktmästare's base colour.
- Balance +5:00: a bar to the right in Vaktmästare's neon.
- Balance −3:00: the number in the base colour; the −10 h end mark and label in the base colour, the +10 h ones in the
  neon, the other marks grey.
- Utveckling on screen: the bar keeps Vaktmästare's colours.
