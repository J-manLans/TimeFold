Today's pie

The day's expected hours as slices beside the status line, for the profile with the balance. Each slice is marked by its border in the base colour while empty, fills in it as its hour is worked, and lights as it clicks in. Past the expected hours a second lap fills in the neon, over the first. Two shapes to compare: pizza slices meeting near the centre, or ring slices like a round health bar. The dashed bar is for the sketch only.
